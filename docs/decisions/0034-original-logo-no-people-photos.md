# ADR-0034: Original logo mark; no people photos

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Source:** Step 0.1 Q7; §7.3

## Decision
Step 1.4 draws an original SVG logo mark and "FITCOACH" wordmark using the lime/charcoal tokens; it also produces the PWA icons in 5.3. Marketing and auth screens use gradients, shapes and illustrations instead of photos of people unless licensed photos are supplied (recorded in `docs/assets.md`).

## Why
No logo file exists, and nothing may be extracted from the design boards.

## Consequences
- The logo can be swapped later by replacing one SVG component and regenerating icons.
